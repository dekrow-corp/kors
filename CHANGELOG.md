# Changelog

All changes to the KORS specification are recorded here, most recent first.

## 0.1, 18 November 2026

First public version.

- Definition of KORS as an architecture property of production applications (ISA-95 Level 3).
- Five criteria: event-based communication, common vocabulary at the boundaries, autonomy, modular adoption, open data.
- Boundary section for non-KORS systems (ERP, machines, existing UNS).
- Conformance rules: all five criteria, self-declared, stated against a named version.
- Checklist, examples, rationale, and what KORS owes to related work.
